# Podcast Feature: Redefining Business with Techno-Humanization – A Conversation with Márcio Bueno

![Podcast Banner](/techno/techno.jpg)

In this compelling episode of our podcast series, hosts Luka and Pedro sit down with **Márcio Bueno**, a visionary leader, founder of **BE&SK**, and **CIO/COO at BS2 Insurance**, to unpack the concept of **Techno-Humanization** — a bold new approach that merges digital transformation with ethical, purpose-driven business design.

With over 15 years of experience leading global teams across Europe and Latin America, Márcio brings both philosophical clarity and practical tools to the table. His mission: help businesses grow profitably while creating social value — or as he puts it, “creating wealth without creating poverty.”

## 🧠 What You'll Learn

- **What is Techno-Humanization?**  
  Márcio defines it as a methodology to apply technology toward building conscious, ethical, and sustainable business models — without sacrificing performance.

- **The Origin Story**  
  Techno-Humanization was born out of frustration. Tired of seeing companies preach inclusion and ESG while practicing exclusion, Márcio created a framework rooted in coherence between speech and action.

- **Case Studies in Real Change**  
  One of the standout stories includes a consumer credit firm that transformed into a financial literacy company, reducing debt dependency and shifting its business model to empowerment. Other clients include major global brands like Volvo, Mondelez, and Nespresso — although Márcio notes that the most profound transformations often remain confidential.

- **The Role of a Dual Leader (CIO & COO)**  
  Márcio offers rare insight into managing both operations and IT, emphasizing the need for technology to serve business — not the other way around. His daily work focuses on people, adaptability, and aligning growth with capacity.

- **Ethics & Artificial Intelligence**  
  Márcio discusses why companies shouldn’t chase AI blindly. Not every organization is ready, and many underestimate the cultural shift required. He also believes humanities graduates — with their ability to ask better questions — may soon outpace tech grads in influence.

- **CSR vs Real Change**  
  Perhaps most provocatively, he challenges corporate social responsibility as it's often practiced. Instead of donating to diabetes campaigns, why not just reduce sugar in your product to begin with? For Márcio, real responsibility is structural, not seasonal.

- **Future of the Methodology**  
  Looking ahead, Márcio hopes Techno-Humanization will be studied in universities, embedded into MBA programs, and potentially adapted to public policy — though he’s skeptical of adoption in developing-world governments in the near term.

## 🌍 Reflections on a Global Career

Márcio also opens up about his 15 years working across Europe and Latin America. He shares how cultural nuance shaped his leadership approach and why multinationals must balance global standards with local sensitivity. His takeaway: **the best leaders are listeners first**.
