# Winning Hack-The-Future 2024 Hackathon in Antwerp

![Team Dinamo at Hack-The-Future 2024](/htf/htf-dinamo.jpg)

In November 2024, my friend Pedro and I embarked on an exhilarating journey at the **Hack-The-Future** hackathon held at the Flanders Meeting & Convention Center in Antwerp. This annual event, organized by the Cronos Group, brings together IT and creative students to tackle real-world challenges in a vibrant, collaborative environment.&#x20;

## 🚀 The Challenge: Speed and Precision

Assigned to the **Exsertus** challenge track, we faced a series of time-based tasks. Each challenge required us to solve problems and submit "flags" specific outputs or solutions. The twist? Our performance was measured by the time taken (often in milliseconds) to submit the correct flag after loading the challenge page. Speed and accuracy were paramount.

## 🧠 Our Strategy: Divide and Conquer

Operating under the team name **Dinamo**, a nod to the popular sports clubs in Eastern Europe, we devised a two-pronged approach:

- **Problem Solving**: I focused on understanding and solving the challenges as efficiently as possible.
- **Performance Optimization**: Pedro took the lead in enhancing our solution's performance. Initially, we used JavaScript for rapid development. However, recognizing the need for speed, Pedro rewrote our solutions in **Go**, leveraging its performance benefits to shave off precious milliseconds.

This strategy allowed us to tackle challenges swiftly while ensuring our solutions were optimized for speed.

## 🥇 Victory and Celebration

Our combined efforts paid off. Team Dinamo emerged as the top performers in our challenge track, securing first place. The event concluded with a delightful ceremony featuring food, drinks, and an opportunity to network with fellow participants and industry professionals. It was a memorable experience, celebrating not just our victory but the spirit of innovation and collaboration that the hackathon fostered.

Beyond the challenges, we took pride in presenting our work through a sleek website we developed during the hackathon. It served as a testament to our problem-solving journey and the solutions we crafted, offering insights into our approach and the technologies we employed.

Participating in Hack-The-Future 2024 was an enriching experience. It challenged us to think on our feet, collaborate effectively, and push the boundaries of our technical skills. The event's dynamic atmosphere, coupled with the opportunity to engage with like-minded individuals, made it an unforgettable adventure.
